What AWD.WIN is

AWD.WIN is a private software product focused on supporting Roblox development workflows.

The product is distributed privately and access is limited to a controlled number of authorized users.

AWD.WIN does not inject code into games, modify Roblox game files, or provide gameplay advantages such as aimbots or wallhacks.
